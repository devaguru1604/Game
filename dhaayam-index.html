📜 How to Play Dhaayam
🎲
Dayakattai Dice
Two dice, each with faces 1, 2, 3, 4, 8. Roll both — their sum is your move value.
🔓
Entering the Board (Dhaayam)
Either die shows 1 → unlock one piece onto the board. You also get an extra roll!
⭐
Extra Turns
Either die shows 1, or sum = 8 (4+4) or 16 (8+8) — you get another turn! Landing a piece at Home also earns an extra turn.
🗺️
Movement
Pieces travel clockwise along the outer ring of the 7×7 board, then inward along their column toward the center (4,4).
🛡️
Safe Squares (✕)
Marked squares are safe — no piece can be captured here.
💥
Capture
Land on an enemy piece (not on a safe square) → their piece goes back home. You get an extra turn!
🏠
Winning
Move all 4 pieces to the center (Home). You need an exact roll to enter. First to do it wins!
📊
