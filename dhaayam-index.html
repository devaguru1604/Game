📜 How to Play Dhaayam
👥
Players
Player 1 (Red 🔴) — You! Click numbers from the LEFT column to move.
Player 2 (AI ⚫) — Computer opponent. Difficulty selectable: Easy, Medium, Hard.
🎲
Number Column
Each column has numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 — each with 4 boxes.
Click a box to move that many steps. Once clicked, that box is permanently blocked for the whole game.
Each number can be used a maximum of 4 times.
🔓
Entering the Board (Dhaayam)
Click 1 to unlock a piece from home onto the board at your entry point.
Player 1 enters at (7,4) — bottom center.
AI enters at (1,4) — top center.
You also get an extra turn!
⭐
Extra Turns
Click 1 (Dhaayam), 6, or 8 → move your piece AND get another turn!
Capturing an enemy piece also gives an extra turn.
Getting a piece to Home (4,4) gives an extra turn + score ×4!
🗺️
Movement Path
Pieces spiral clockwise inward — outer ring → middle ring → inner ring → center.
P1: (7,4) → right → up → left → down → spiral inward → (4,5) → (4,4).
You cannot move from one safe square directly to another safe square.
Required: You MUST capture at least one opponent's piece before entering your inner ring!
🛡️
Safe Squares (✕)
Pieces on safe squares cannot be captured.
You also cannot move from one safe square to another safe square in one step.
💥
Capture
Land on an enemy piece (not on a safe square) → their piece goes back home and their score decreases by the steps that piece had moved.
You get an extra turn!
📊
Score
Score increases by the number of steps you move each turn.
When a piece reaches Home (4,4) → your score is multiplied by 4 ×!
When your piece is captured → score decreases by that piece's steps.
🏠
Winning
Move all 4 pieces to the center (4,4). You need an exact count to enter home — no overshooting!
First player to get all 4 pieces home wins!
🤖
AI Difficulty
Easy — AI picks random numbers and moves randomly.
Medium — AI plays smart 70% of the time, random 30%.
Hard — AI always picks the best move: prefers captures, avoids danger, advances strategically.
